# Exam-prep question and source map

Three short sets appear before the broader lecture practice. Each has five questions, mixes multiple choice and numerical input, and targets 3–5 minutes. Together they cover the same ten concept checks and five calculation formulas used in Homework 3 and Mock Quizzes 2A/2B. Prompts are newly written; calculation inputs differ from the source examples.

| Set | Item | Type | Focus | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | Multiple choice | Geometric average return | Homework 3, Q1 |
| 1 | 2 | Multiple choice | Standard deviation / volatility | Homework 3, Q2 |
| 1 | 3 | Multiple choice | Diversifiable risk | Homework 3, Q5 |
| 1 | 4 | Number | Market risk premium: 11 − 3 = **8%** | Homework 3, Q11 |
| 1 | 5 | Number | CAPM: 3 + 1.5 × (11 − 3) = **15%** | Homework 3, Q12 |
| 2 | 1 | Multiple choice | Semi-strong efficiency | Mock Quiz 2A, Q3 |
| 2 | 2 | Multiple choice | News relative to expectations | Mock Quiz 2A, Q4 |
| 2 | 3 | Multiple choice | Beta as market sensitivity | Mock Quiz 2A, Q7 |

| 2 | 5 | Number | MM II: 10 + 0.5 × (10 − 4) = **13%** | Mock Quiz 2A, Q13 |
| 3 | 1 | Multiple choice | CAPM prices systematic risk | Mock Quiz 2B, Q6 |
| 3 | 2 | Multiple choice | Project-specific discount rates | Mock Quiz 2B, Q8 |
| 3 | 3 | Multiple choice | Deductible interest reduces taxable income | Mock Quiz 2B, Q10 |
| 3 | 4 | Number | WACC: 0.5 × 14 + 0.5 × 8 × (1 − 0.25) = **10%** | Mock Quiz 2B, Q14 |
| 3 | 5 | Number | Permanent-debt tax shield: 0.20 × 80 = **$16 million** | Mock Quiz 2B, Q15 |

MM I holds investment policy and operating cash flows fixed in perfect capital markets without taxes. MM II explicitly uses D/E and the no-tax formula. WACC uses market-value weights and a fully usable interest deduction. The tax-shield PV exercise states permanent debt, sufficient taxable income, and tax-shield risk equal to debt risk.

The app states: “The lecture practice below covers a broader range of material. Not every lecture question or topic will be on the exam. Use the homework, Quiz 2A/2B, and your instructor’s exam guidance to focus your preparation.” These sets do not claim to be a complete exam blueprint.

Only the named practice materials were used for the prep questions. The actual exam and archived exam versions were not used. Original TeX/PDF files are not included in the public site.
